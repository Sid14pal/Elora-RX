Semaglutide is a GLP-1 receptor agonist that works with the body's natural appetite-regulating processes. It may help reduce hunger, increase feelings of fullness, and support weight management alongside balanced nutrition, physical activity, and healthy lifestyle habits.

Semaglutide works by mimicking the glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) hormone that naturally signals fullness to the brain after meals. It gently moderates stomach emptying, dampens appetite, reduces intense cravings, and assists in steady metabolic regulation.

Most patients begin noticing a reduction in appetite and food noise within the first 1 to 2 weeks. Measurable weight loss typically becomes evident within 4 to 8 weeks as your body reaches an optimal therapeutic dose, with clinical studies demonstrating sustained results alongside balanced nutrition and active lifestyle habits.

The most common side effects are mild and temporary, such as slight nausea, digestive changes, or mild fatigue as your body adapts. These are minimized by starting at a gentle 0.25 mg starter dose and gradually stepping up over time. Your dedicated clinical team is available anytime to adjust your protocol.
